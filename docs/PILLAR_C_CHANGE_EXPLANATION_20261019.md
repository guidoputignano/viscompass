# Pillar C — explaining expenditure change

## Delivered scope

The portfolio dossier now opens its change analysis with a concise accounting reading: chronological net change; positive contributions and offsetting negative contributions; the three largest products on each side and the remaining amount; and continuing, entered and exited product components. A zero balance with offsetting movements is distinguished from no observations. Negative adjustments retain their sign even when an entry reduces spending or an exit increases it.

Named products open the existing searchable two-period detail. The bridge starts at zero and ends at the net change, not at closing expenditure. Product presence is defined within the selected portfolio and aggregated organization/channel scope. It is not market entry, switching or a count of patients. Shared products remain outside this single-portfolio bridge.

## Price, quantity and mix evidence

The dossier reuses the already published, hash-pinned R5 cost snapshot through its existing authenticated endpoint. It neither changes the frozen workbook nor introduces new conversions, classifications or data imports.

Portfolio effects are available only for the aggregate of the four organizations, all channels, FY2024–FY2025. Both annual endpoint selections retain that chronological comparison. The frozen portfolio table cannot be applied to an individual organization, channel or matched January–May period.

Admission follows ML-A16: below 50% coverage in either period, show support only; from 50% to below 80%, show partial effects; at least 80% remains an admitted subset, not whole-portfolio coverage. Imputed-unit outputs never open admission. Coverage uses absolute expenditure of continuing AIC × organization × channel strata. Its denominator and admitted expenditure change are separate from the dossier's aggregated product-presence bridge.

The three additive effects are quantity, mix and unit cost from the same Shapley decomposition. LMDI is not mixed in as a fourth contribution. Cached-value residuals have a predeclared EUR 0.000001 consistency guard; separate cent-rounding differences remain visible and no effect is altered to force a displayed identity.

The real-route figures retain the qualification that unit conversion is estimated from AIFA registry and ledger conventions, not institutionally confirmed. The independently checked numerical R5 receipt does not upgrade frozen verification wording, clinical mapping or whole-workbook acceptance. These effects do not establish negotiated net prices, realized savings or patient growth.
